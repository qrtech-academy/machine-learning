\chapter{Worked Solutions}
\label{sol:ch:solutions}

This appendix holds the solutions to the exercises that are answered on paper: the Calculation
exercises, where a model is trained or an image traced by hand, and the Reflection exercises, where
the answer is a sentence or two of reasoning. Each solution is headed with the number of the
exercise it answers and the page that exercise is on, and every step is shown, so a slip can be
traced to the line it was made on.

The solutions to the Code exercises are not here. They are published in the companion repository,
lecture by lecture, and each chapter's exercises say where.

Work each exercise before you read its solution. Most of these calculations are short, and all of
them are much easier to follow than to produce; the point of doing one is finding out which step you
could not have produced.
